# Product Thesis

The Finance & Assurance Platform is a synthetic digital twin for enterprise finance and assurance.

It models the full lifecycle of financial information:

```text
business event
-> source-system record
-> accounting interpretation
-> control and assurance result
-> governance treatment
-> reporting or planning use
-> decision event
```

The design goal is not to create separate finance, audit, governance, and planning products. The design goal is to prove that these capabilities can sit on one coherent substrate while preserving clear domain ownership.

## Core Claim

Financial truth is produced through events, interpreted through accounting rules, tested through controls, governed through evidence, and only then permitted to drive decisions.

## Product Lenses

- Hermes asks: where did this information come from, and did it arrive completely?
- Atlas asks: what do the books say, and are they mechanically and economically coherent?
- Argus asks: what assurance tests passed, failed, or require review?
- Aegis asks: who owns the issue, what is the treatment, and what can be relied on?
- Pythia asks: which governed actuals are safe enough to inform future decisions?

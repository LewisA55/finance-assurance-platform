# Excel v0.2 review model

The workbook consumes `Q-FINANCE-C2@v1` with detached digest
`sha256:7538132e08ea2b7e8c20f339db10e6c138cd2a08c71c4708c47c378d7c1b0141`.
Its reporting scope is Nexus Group in GBP, with 66 monthly actuals through
`RV-NEXUS-GROUP-2026-06@v1` and 60 forecast months starting July 2026.
Historical actuals are retained in source minor units. Statement links convert
them to GBP for modelling. The plan extracts retain the three C2 scenarios
aggregated by month and statement line before workbook calculation.

Control holds the case selector, reporting basis, model map and links to the
working sheets. The Statements tab contains one forecast. Assumptions uses the
case selected on Control to pick the C2 plan and editable drivers. A monthly
revenue adjustment can vary subscription and services revenue from the selected
plan. Operating expenses can transition to a percentage of revenue over 36
months. Receivables and payables use a 30-day month. Net PPE
rolls forward with cash capex, 36-month depreciation on opening PPE and
60-month depreciation on new capex starting the month after purchase. Interest
uses opening debt and lease balances. Cash taxes apply to positive pretax
profit, with no forecast tax-loss utilisation or deferred tax recognition.
Debt principal amortises monthly and the revolver draws up to its opening
capacity to support the cash floor. Existing leases and specified non-operating
balances carry forward. The model assumes no new equity, dividends, buybacks,
lease originations or FX effects in the forecast. A negative cash projection
after the facility is exhausted represents an unmet financing need.

The DCF uses month-end unlevered free cash flow and effective annual WACC.
Terminal cash flow is the final 12 forecast months, grown at the selected
perpetual rate and discounted from month 60. The equity bridge subtracts
cutover term debt and lease liabilities and adds cutover cash. Funding gaps,
non-positive terminal free cash flow and WACC at or below terminal growth
block the displayed valuation. Workbook assumptions and outputs are local
planning analysis with their own calculation basis. C2 approval refers to
source planning inputs and does not approve the workbook's scenario results.

The builder verifies the pinned package seal, exact monthly reporting versions,
scope and financial controls. The Checks tab independently ties balance sheet,
cash, earnings, PPE, debt and revolver capacity across the model, and compares
historical control totals with the source extracts. Its summary shows the
failed-check count, largest absolute difference, first failed month and
missing results. The model never reads this diagnostic summary as an input.

The builder uses a Codex workspace Node runtime with `@oai/artifact-tool` and
`jszip` available from the local `node_modules`. The standard web dependencies
do not install the workbook renderer. From the repository root, with those
packages available:

```powershell
node consumer-products/excel/build-model.mjs `
  build/finance-delivery/Q-FINANCE-C2-V1 `
  consumer-products/excel/work/Nexus-Financial-Model-rebuild.xlsx `
  consumer-products/excel/exports/review
```

The builder refuses an existing output path. Verify and review the workbook
before publishing a tagged release asset.
